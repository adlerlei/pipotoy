## 關於 HeianPipo

某天，來自遙遠銀河系的 **HeianPipo** 決定帶著一群小跟班來地球玩。
這群傢伙分別來自八顆不同的星球——Grumglo、Drippus、Yumblax、Glorfen、Schmerbia、Blobia、Flumpor、Dunkvon——每顆星球的生物都長得奇形怪狀，名字更是讓地球人唸了三遍還唸不對。

只是，他們剛到地球，還沒適應這裡的大氣。
為了保護自己，這些小傢伙全都進入了防禦模式——把身上的顏色壓低、調柔，偽裝成地球上最安靜、最無害的樣子。

所以你現在看到的那些粉粉的、霧霧的、奶油色的、灰灰的顏色？
那不是本色，那是保護色。

*等他們真正適應地球那天，才是好戲上場的時候。*

---

*One day, **HeianPipo** arrived from a distant galaxy with a ragtag crew of followers — creatures from eight different planets: Grumglo, Drippus, Yumblax, Glorfen, Schmerbia, Blobia, Flumpor, and Dunkvon. Each species looked stranger than the last, with names no earthling could pronounce on the first try.*

*But they'd just landed. Earth's atmosphere was unfamiliar, and until they adjusted, every one of them switched into defensive mode — muting their colors, going soft and quiet, blending into the most harmless shades they could manage.*

*So those muted pastels, those foggy creams, those dusty greys?*
*That's not their true form. That's camouflage.*

*When they finally adapt to Earth — that's when things get interesting.*

## 關於 Mistoria · 霧紗星

當 HeianPipo 帶著八星球的小跟班來到地球時，還有 15 位安靜的訪客也一起來了。他們來自意識維度更高的星球——**Mistoria · 霧紗星**——外表柔軟、近乎透明，像和紙過濾過的光。

他們不參與防禦色的偽裝。他們本來就是這樣的存在。

他們默默地幫助人類，守在每個角落，也看顧著八星球的跟班們安睡在保護色裡。

沒人知道他們在等什麼。但他們知道：總有一天，pipo 會遇到大哥。在那一天到來之前，他們什麼都不說，只是把世界輕輕擦亮一點。

---

*When HeianPipo arrived on Earth with his eight-planet crew, fifteen quiet visitors came too. They are from a planet of higher consciousness — **Mistoria** — soft, nearly translucent, like light filtered through washi paper.*

*They take no part in the camouflage. They have always been this way.*

*They help humans in silence, watching over every corner, keeping the eight-planet creatures safe in their sleeping disguise.*

*No one knows what they're waiting for. But they know: one day, pipo will meet the Elder. Until then, they say nothing — they just brighten the world, very gently.*

## 關於 PipoToy

PipoToy 是一套為「AI 時代」設計的全新 UI Toy Kit。
語法像玩具，樣式像藝術，拿起來就能玩。

*PipoToy is a brand-new UI Toy Kit designed for the AI era.
Playful syntax, beautiful style — just pick it up and play.*

以前學做網頁，要先學 HTML、CSS、JavaScript，光入門就要幾個月，很多人還沒開始就放棄了。PipoToy 想把這道門檻徹底拆掉。複雜的底層交給 AI 和框架處理，你只需要告訴它你要什麼，它就幫你做好。

*Learning web development used to mean months of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript before you could build anything. PipoToy tears down that barrier. The complex parts are handled by AI and the framework underneath — you just say what you want, and it's done.*

不需要寫 CSS，不需要懂排版，不需要任何程式經驗。
無論你是初學者、小朋友、設計師，還是從來沒碰過程式的人——PipoToy 都是為你做的。

*No CSS. No layout knowledge. No coding experience required.
Whether you're a beginner, a child, a designer, or someone who has never touched code — PipoToy is made for you.*

---

基於 Web Components 原生標準，採用宣告式 HTML 語法。至於顏色——HeianPipo 想打造有趣的工具，需要顏色，就順手把這群小跟班拿來用了。他們現在是色票。

*Built on native Web Components, declarative HTML syntax. As for the colors — HeianPipo needed a palette to build something fun, so he did what any reasonable intergalactic traveler would do: he turned his little followers into swatches. They are the color system now.*
