v0.1.7-alpha June 15, 2026 · 2026 年 6 月 15 日 Alpha
✦ Added · 新增
🫳
New <pipo-tap> component — a clay-soft button that floats when you hover and squishes when you press. One tag, eight optional attributes, no CSS.
新元件 <pipo-tap> —— 黏土感按鈕，滑過會浮起、按下會壓扁。一個標籤、8 個可選屬性，不用寫 CSS。
🔀
Same tag becomes a real <button> or <a> link depending on href; target="blank" opens links in a new tab (with safe rel); two hover animations (float / tilt), four sizes, pill / square shapes, full-width, and disabled states.
同一個標籤依 href 自動變成真的 <button> 或 <a> 連結;target="blank" 可開新分頁(自動補安全的 rel);兩種滑過動畫(float / tilt)、四種大小、膠囊 / 方形、滿版與停用狀態。
📖
A full bilingual teaching page with live, pressable demos for every attribute site/examples/pipo-tap.html
完整雙語教學頁,每個屬性都有真的能按的 live demo site/examples/pipo-tap.html
✦ Changed · 變更
🃏
The <pipo-tap> card in the components overview is now live and clickable, no longer "Coming Soon".
元件總覽裡的 <pipo-tap> 卡片正式啟用、可以點了,不再是「Coming Soon」。
✦ Removed · 移除
🧹
Cleaned up a leftover [data-theme="light"] selector — there was never a dark theme; the site is light-only.
清掉遺留的 [data-theme="light"] 選擇器 —— 從來就沒有深色主題,全站固定淺色。
